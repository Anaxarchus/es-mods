name "Corporations"
version "0.1"
authors
	"Anaxarchus"
about "A stock exchange for human space. About forty companies, from Betelgeuse Shipyards and Kraz Cybernetics to new firms that rise and fall, bid for contracts across the galaxy; their profits move their share prices. Trade shares at any planet with a bank, collect dividends, and watch for buyouts, bankruptcies and antitrust breakups. Shipyards keep stock: each human hull is restocked by the company that owns its design, NPC captains buy hulls faster where ships are being lost, and governments put warships out to tender for any shipbuilder to win. Needs the Dado mod loader (mod API 9 or newer) in the game's modloaders folder."
