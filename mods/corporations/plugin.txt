name "Corporations"
version "0.4.1"
authors
	"Anaxarchus"
about "A stock exchange for human space. About forty companies, from billion-credit names like Betelgeuse Shipyards and Kraz Cybernetics to new firms worth a million, bid for contracts across the galaxy, research new technology, raise money and grow; their profits move their share prices. Trade shares at any planet with a bank, collect dividends, and watch for buyouts, bankruptcies and antitrust breakups. Shipyards keep stock: each human hull is restocked by the company that owns its design, NPC captains buy hulls faster where ships are being lost, and governments put warships out to tender for any shipbuilder to win. Hulls bound for dangerous space travel in convoys you can escort or, for a rival, intercept; strikes and accidents raise commodity prices near a company's home; builders take orders for new hulls at their home planets, sooner and cheaper for captains they trust. Every company's chief executive comes from one pool of talent, hired, poached and voted out on their record (you can hire one too); a large enough stake gives you a board vote; sell short, buy tips (at a risk), found a company of your own in any industry and build it up (or buy out its rivals), and refit outfits into your hulls. Needs the Dado mod loader (mod API 9 or newer) in the game's modloaders folder."
