name "Corporations"
version "0.1"
authors
	"Anaxarchus"
about "A stock exchange for human space. About forty companies, from Betelgeuse Shipyards and Kraz Cybernetics to new firms that rise and fall, bid for contracts across the galaxy; their profits move their share prices. Trade shares at any planet with a bank, collect dividends, and watch for buyouts, bankruptcies and antitrust breakups. Shipyards keep stock: each human hull is restocked by the company that owns its design, NPC captains buy hulls faster where ships are being lost, and governments put warships out to tender for any shipbuilder to win. Hulls bound for dangerous space travel in convoys you can escort or, for a rival, intercept; strikes and accidents raise commodity prices near a company's home; builders take orders for new hulls at their home planets, sooner and cheaper for captains they trust. Every company has a chief executive; a large enough stake gives you a board vote; sell short, buy tips (at a risk), charter a company of your own, and refit outfits into your hulls. Needs the Dado mod loader (mod API 9 or newer) in the game's modloaders folder."
