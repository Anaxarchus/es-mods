# MOPS operations: repeatable job-board jobs for contractors ("mops: contractor",
# set by "MOPS 2: Contract"). Each one sends the player from a human spaceport to
# kill a hostile alien force in the systems where vanilla puts that species, then
# land back at the job's planet to be paid.
#
# Rarity. Vanilla's "Spacediving professionals" job is `random < 14`. Every MOPS
# job is rarer than that on its own (`random < 10`, the far-perimeter ones
# `random < 8`), and each is only offered from spaceports near its own border:
# most job boards can roll one or two of them, a few border hubs (Zaurak, between
# Sumar, Polaris and Durax) up to four, and only for the most senior contractors.
#
# Difficulty. Tiers are gated by combat rank (rank = floor(ln "combat rating"):
# rank 6 >= 404, rank 7 >= 1097, rank 8 >= 2981) and, for the heavier ones, by
# "mops: operations" (completed MOPS jobs). A job is never offered while the
# player is not hostile with that government (e.g. after making peace with the
# Unfettered or the Korath through their storylines).
#
#   job                                  rank  ops  pay        targets
#   Unfettered Incursion                  5+    -   1,500,000  Unfettered Raid + Large Unfettered (Hai border)
#   Unfettered War Band                   7+    3   3,500,000  two waves across the Ultima Thule wormhole and Sumar
#   Korath Raiders (Polaris)              6+    -   2,400,000  Korath Large Raid + Korath Raid near Polaris
#   Korath Strip-Mining (Durax)           7+    3   4,000,000  Korath Miners + 2 Large Raids at Durax, a second wing nearby
#   Alpha Cell (South)                    7+    3   3,000,000  Alpha warships in the southern rim; after the FW campaign
#   Drone War Spillover (Korath sector)   8+    5   5,500,000  Kor Sestor and Kor Mereti fleets; once visited
#   Graveyard Sweep (Ka'het)              8+    5   6,000,000  three Ka'het war groups; once visited


# --- Unfettered Hai: the Hai/human border (Sumar, Cardax and the Ultima Thule wormhole) ---

mission "MOPS Job: Unfettered Incursion"
	name "Perimeter maintenance: Hai border"
	description `MOPS reports an Unfettered Hai raiding party, led by the <npc>, operating in human space along the Hai border (Sumar, Cardax, Rajak, Hassaleh, Danoa or Ultima Thule). Destroy all of them by <date> and return to <destination>. Payment: <payment>. MOPS reminds contractors that it does not exist.`
	job
	repeat
	deadline 20
	to offer
		has "mops: contractor"
		"combat rating" >= 149
		"reputation: Hai (Unfettered)" < 0
		random < 10
	source
		near "Sumar" 0 5
		government "Republic" "Free Worlds" "Syndicate" "Neutral" "Independent"
		attributes "spaceport"
	npc kill
		government "Hai (Unfettered)"
		personality heroic staying vindictive marked target tracked
		system
			system "Sumar" "Cardax" "Rajak" "Hassaleh" "Danoa" "Ultima Thule"
		fleet "Unfettered Raid"
		fleet "Large Unfettered"
		dialog `The last Unfettered ship is gone. Your comm chimes with an automated MOPS message: "Containment confirmed. Return to <destination> for settlement. This message will be deleted from our records shortly and from yours, if you'd be so kind."`
	on visit
		dialog `You've returned to <planet>, but the Unfettered raiding party is still out there. MOPS does not pay for partial suppression. MOPS barely pays for complete suppression, legally speaking.`
	on fail
		dialog `The Unfettered raiders were still at large when your MOPS contract lapsed. The Office has reassigned the operation to "a backup contractor," and notes that the backup contractor is also you, next time.`
	on complete
		payment 1500000
		"mops: operations" ++
		dialog phrase "mops: settlement"

# The settlement message every MOPS job ends with.
phrase "mops: settlement"
	word
		`You land and find <payment> already in your account, wired from `
	word
		"a municipal drainage authority"
		"a company that manufactures commemorative plates"
		"the pension fund of a retired admiral who does not exist"
		"a charity for distressed spacedock cats"
		"a sanitation contractor whose only listed employee is you"
		"an account labelled PERIMETER (MISC.)"
	word
		`. `
	word
		`Attached is a form acknowledging receipt, which you are asked to sign and then destroy.`
		`Attached is a satisfaction survey. Question one asks whether you are still alive. There is no space to answer "no."`
		`Attached is a note from Registrar Vosk: "Your next of kin have been informed that you are, for now, fine. They seemed surprised."`
		`Attached is a message from Prell: "Splendid work. Officially, of course, there was no work."`
		`Attached is a reminder that MOPS contractors are not covered by any treaty, convention, or health plan.`
		`Attached is a commendation, which is also a non-disclosure agreement, which is also a receipt.`
		`Attached is a brief notice that your file has been moved from "expendable" to "expendable (preferred)."`
