# The Racing League: the intro mission line. A short, light-hearted way into
# the leagues: the Honourable Peregrine Wexley-Thorne, who has spent his
# inheritance, talks the player into sponsoring the revival of his family's
# old racing team, scouts four people across human space with them, wins a
# place in the B League, and then leaves for his childless great-uncle's
# estate on Hestia, signing the team over to the player.
#
# The mod (src/Intro.dados, src/racing.dados) sets "racing: owns team" (1 when
# the player owns a team) and "racing: slot free" (1 while the B League's
# reserved place is free) every day; the line is offered only to a player
# with a million credits, no team and a free place. Each leg fails, and the
# sponsorship is paid back, if the player buys a team (or the place is
# taken) on the way. The finale sets "racing: intro done" to 1; the mod then
# revives Wexley-Thorne Racing in the reserved place with the four signed on
# five-season contracts and sets it to 2 (or to 3, the sponsorship refunded,
# if it could not after all).
#
# The legs: anywhere in human space (the bar) -> Valhalla (the engineer) ->
# Bourne (the young pilot) -> Martini (the second pilot) -> Foundry (the pit
# crew chief) -> Luna (the league's registry) -> Hestia (the great-uncle).
