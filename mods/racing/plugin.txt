Buy a franchise, hire pilots, engineers and crew from a pool of named people with track records, fund research on your racing ship, sign sponsors, and climb by promotion; or watch a bad team eat your savings. With Corporations and the Racing League and Corporations plugin installed, the exchange's companies are the sponsors."
