# The ships of the Shoal. The Wardens reuse the Kor Mereti drone sprites (and the
# Kor Sestor battlecruiser for the Undertow), with the hardpoints of those ships.
# Most of their strength is built into the hull (generation, shields, repair,
# cooling) rather than into outfits. Their weapons and engines are unplunderable
# "Warden ..." copies of stock outfits (in "shoal outfits.txt"), so what a
# disabled Warden gives up is its materials: Shoal Glass, Warden Cores and Deep
# Lattice. They are uncapturable and never self-destruct, so a disabled Warden
# can always be plundered.
#
# v0.3 (DESIGN.md section 15): rebalanced for ships fitted with Shoal Mk III
# gear. Shields, hull, regeneration and the Warden weapon copies' damage went
# up; energy generation and cooling x1.12 to feed the copies; loot unchanged.
#
# v0.5.0 (DESIGN.md section 22): cut down so a solo captain with Shoal Mk I/II
# gear can sink them. These are the shallowest tier; the deeper tiers are
# generated variants ("Warden Picket 2" ... "The Undertow 4", in "shoal deep
# wardens.txt", tools/deep/gen.py). Shields and hull: Picket and Lancer -45%,
# Bastion -50%, Undertow -66%; regeneration -40% / -40% / -50% / -66%; the
# weapon copies -30% (all of them); the Bastion trades a Husk-Slice turret for
# a second Warder, the Undertow leaves a gun port and two turret mounts empty
# and carries less energy. More glass on every hull; cores and lattice as
# before.
#
# The drowned hulls are stock human ships, a little worse for the water: less
# hull, less shield, a touch more drag, short-handed (required crew is 0.8 of the
# stock hull's, rounded), stripped down to a reactor, engines and a
# hyperdrive. They are real ships and can be captured and flown. The variants
# set how much glass (and anything else) each wreck carries.
