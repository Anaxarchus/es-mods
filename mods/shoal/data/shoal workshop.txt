# The Shoal: the workshop at Breaker's Rest.
#
# Since v0.3 (DESIGN.md section 15) the workshop builds only the Warden Steward
# crew line (DESIGN.md sections 12 and 14). The twelve exotic lines are no
# longer crafted: Mk I and Mk II come only from drowned wrecks and salvage
# leads, and Mk III only by upgrading at the Breakers' Yard (the mod's code).
# Every recipe takes refined materials only (Refined Shoal Glass, Refined
# Warden Core) plus Deep Lattice, never raw glass or cores; raw materials are
# refined at Breaker's Rest first (see "shoal refinery.txt").
# Accepting the job is the purchase: "on accept" takes the inputs, charges the credits,
# hands over the outfit and then fails the job so it leaves no entry in the
# mission list. Engine behaviour this relies on (checked in source):
#	- Mission::CanAccept() runs MissionAction::CanBeDone() on "on accept",
#	  which refuses when credits < -payment, or when an outfit with a negative
#	  count is not available in that number installed on the flagship plus in
#	  the (pooled, when landed) cargo. The job then shows as unavailable.
#	- GameAction::Do() removes negative outfits (cargo first, then the
#	  flagship) before giving positive ones, so a Mk I installed on the
#	  flagship frees its slot for the Mk II.
#	- "fail" with no value in "on accept" fails this mission;
#	  PlayerInfo::AcceptJob() then removes it at once (no "on fail" here, so
#	  nothing else happens).
#	- "outfit (all): <name>" is provided in PlayerInfo.cpp (all ships, cargo
#	  and planetary storage).

mission "Shoal: Workshop Warden Steward Mk I"
	name "Workshop: Warden Steward Mk I"
	job
	repeat
	source "Breaker's Rest"
	destination "Breaker's Rest"
	description "The Breakers will build you a Warden Steward Mk I from drowned-fleet salvage. Price: 2 Refined Shoal Glass and 1 Refined Warden Core (in your hold, in storage here, or installed on your flagship), and 100,000 credits. The outfit is handed over as soon as you accept."
	on accept
		outfit "Refined Shoal Glass" -2
		outfit "Refined Warden Core" -1
		payment -100000
		outfit "Warden Steward Mk I" 1
		dialog `The Breakers set a Warden Core in a cradle of glass and wire it into a ship's control boards. After a long silence, the reactor readout steadies and the engine trim lights blink green, all on their own. Nobody is quite comfortable being in the room with it.`
		"shoal: crafted" ++
		fail

mission "Shoal: Workshop Warden Steward Mk II"
	name "Workshop: Warden Steward Mk II"
	job
	repeat
	source "Breaker's Rest"
	destination "Breaker's Rest"
	description "The Breakers will rebuild your Warden Steward Mk I into a Warden Steward Mk II. Price: the Warden Steward Mk I and 2 Refined Warden Cores (in your hold, in storage here, or installed on your flagship), and 500,000 credits. The outfit is handed over as soon as you accept."
	to offer
		"outfit (all): Warden Steward Mk I" > 0
	on accept
		outfit "Warden Steward Mk I" -1
		outfit "Refined Warden Core" -2
		payment -500000
		outfit "Warden Steward Mk II" 1
		dialog `The new Steward is threaded through a test hull from bridge to engine room. Doors open as the Breakers approach them, and the damage-control boards light up before anyone reports a fault. One of them mutters that it is very polite, for a thing that used to try to kill them.`
		"shoal: crafted" ++
		fail

mission "Shoal: Workshop Warden Steward Mk III"
	name "Workshop: Warden Steward Mk III"
	job
	repeat
	source "Breaker's Rest"
	destination "Breaker's Rest"
	description "The Breakers will rebuild your Warden Steward Mk II into a Warden Steward Mk III. Price: the Warden Steward Mk II, 3 Refined Warden Cores and 1 Deep Lattice (in your hold, in storage here, or installed on your flagship), and 2,000,000 credits. The outfit is handed over as soon as you accept."
	to offer
		"outfit (all): Warden Steward Mk II" > 0
	on accept
		outfit "Warden Steward Mk II" -1
		outfit "Refined Warden Core" -3
		outfit "Deep Lattice" -1
		payment -2000000
		outfit "Warden Steward Mk III" 1
		dialog `When the Deep Lattice goes in, every system in the test hull wakes at once. The Steward runs the whole ship for a day without a single human hand on the controls. The Breakers hand it over carefully, and remind you that it still cannot fight off a boarding party.`
		"shoal: crafted" ++
		fail
